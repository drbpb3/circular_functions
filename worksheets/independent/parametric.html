Parametric curves

In a parametric curve, \(x\) and \(y\) are both given in terms of a third variable, \(t\). As \(t\) changes, the point \((x, y)\) traces out a curve.

How to Navigate:

  • Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move between pages
  • Use swiping gestures on touch devices
  • Hover near the bottom of the page to reveal navigation buttons

On each curve page, move forward to fill in the table one column at a time and plot each point. When the table is complete, move forward once more to watch the curve being traced.
